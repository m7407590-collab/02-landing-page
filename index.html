Since 2016

Started in a garage, moved twice, still crowded.

Marginalia opened as a folding table of secondhand paperbacks at a Sunday market. Nine years on, we're a proper shop at VaniVihar.

We stock new releases alongside carefully chosen used copies, and we assure you we stock books beyond your imagination.
